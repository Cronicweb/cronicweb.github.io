Capabilities

Everything the brief asks for

Mapped directly from the project requirements — operations, CRM/marketing and the AI experience layer.

🛎️

Hotel Management

Online booking engine for all 24 rooms with live availability, self check-in and housekeeping dashboard.

🍔

Food Ordering & Delivery

Dedicated ordering for prime customers plus Zomato & third-party aggregator integration.

📲

Captain Order System

Tablet ordering with table QR; orders flow straight to kitchen display screens (KDS).

📦

Inventory Management

Real-time kitchen stock and department-wise inventory, usage, wastage & auto-purchase alerts.

🧾

Billing & GST

Automated invoicing with auto GST calculation, split invoices and GST e-invoicing.

📊

Tally Accounting

Seamless import of sales, purchase and stock into Tally with verify-only posting.

🎉

Event Offers

Manage New Year, Christmas & festival campaigns with targeted offers.

🎟️

Coupon Sales

Generate and manually sell promotional coupons with redemption tracking.

🎂

Customer Reminders

Automated WhatsApp/SMS/email for birthdays, anniversaries & special dates.

🗂️

Customer Database

Centralized 360° profile tracking past visits, spend and preferences.

🔀

Cross-Promotions

System suggests cross-sells — e.g. a cinema discount pushed to a hotel guest.

🤖

AI Experience

QR ordering, AI dish search and fully automated self-ordering with live stock & pricing.

Delivery plan

Phased rollout

A pragmatic sequence — get revenue-critical operations live first, then layer on the AI experience and future amenities.

Phase 1
0–3 mo

Core operations

Hotel booking, F&B billing with GST, captain orders + KDS, inventory and Tally integration.

Phase 2
3–5 mo

CRM & marketing

Customer database, reminders, event offers, coupons and cross-promotions across floors.

Phase 3
5–8 mo

AI customer experience

QR table ordering, AI menu search and fully automated self-ordering with live stock checks.

Phase 4
8 mo+

Future amenities

Gym & swimming-pool memberships, loyalty expansion and advanced analytics.
