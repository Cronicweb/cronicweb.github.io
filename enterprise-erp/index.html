01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

One operating model.
Two connected cycles.

Map the hand-offs between commercial, finance, procurement and warehouse teams. Standardize how transactions move, who can act, and how exceptions are surfaced.

Order-to-Cash

From demand to collected cash

Connect customer and item data to order approval, fulfillment, billing and payment visibility.

Procure-to-Pay

From sourcing to settled supplier

Coordinate vendor onboarding, purchasing approvals, receipt matching, bill review and payment controls.

06 / IMPLEMENTATION METHOD

Design before configuration.

A phased delivery path keeps business decisions, system behavior and acceptance evidence linked together.

01 — ALIGNRequirementsStakeholders, goals, scope and acceptance criteria
02 — DISCOVERAS-ISCurrent process, pain points, data and controls
03 — DESIGNTO-BEFuture state, roles, exceptions and record model
04 — BUILDConfigure / customizeNative workflows first; targeted SuiteScript where needed
05 — CONNECTIntegrationContracts, authentication, retries and reconciliation
06 — PROVETesting & UATEnd-to-end evidence, sign-off, training and release readiness
