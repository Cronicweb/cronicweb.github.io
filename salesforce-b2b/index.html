Automation design

Salesforce Flow examples

Record-triggered and scheduled Flow ideas mapped to business events.

Lead routing

Assign by territory, product interest and capacity; notify the owner.

Qualification checks

Require fit, need, authority and timing fields before conversion.

Opportunity stage actions

Create next-step tasks and reminders as stage changes.

Approval routing

Send discount or non-standard terms for authorized review.

Closed Won onboarding

Create an onboarding hand-off and notify the customer team.

Exception notifications

Alert the owner when required data or follow-up is missing.

Conceptual automation patterns only. No Salesforce Flow is deployed in an org.
Data model

Objects & relationships

Illustrative standard-object model for a B2B sales process.

Lead

Prospect details, source, status, rating and qualification.

Account

Company profile, segment, territory and customer relationship.

Contact

People associated with an account and buying group.

Opportunity

Potential revenue, close date, stage, products and forecast category.

Page layouts, fields, validation rules, record types and relationships are design examples, not deployed metadata.
